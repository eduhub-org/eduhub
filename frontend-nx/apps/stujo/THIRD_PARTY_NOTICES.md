Glyphicons Halflings were created by Jan Kovařík and are distributed with
Bootstrap. See https://getbootstrap.com/docs/3.4/components/#glyphicons.

The `unlocked` path was extracted from the IcoMoon font bundled with the
original StuJo Rails application.
