A **Session** is one scheduled meeting of a course, with a start and an end. The Session with the earliest start is the **first Session**; the Session reminder email and the deadline for cancelling depend on it.

## Adding and changing Sessions

On the course's manage page, open the **Dates** tab (for events: **Programme**) and click **Add Session**. For each Session you set the date and time, a title, the location and, if you like, **External Speakers**, whom you find by their email address.

You can also let the [Assistant](assistant.md) draft the Sessions, as long as nobody has registered for the course yet.

## Session Locations

A Session takes place at all locations of the course, unless you remove one for that Session, for example a Session that is exceptionally online only. Click **Remove … for this session**; the location then shows **Not taking place here this session**, and **Add back** restores it. Every Session keeps at least one location.

## Mandatory and optional Sessions

Sessions are mandatory by default. To make a Session optional, turn off its **Mandatory** switch; the row then shows **Optional**. Missing an optional Session does not count. Only mandatory Sessions count towards attendance and certificates. At least one Session must stay mandatory.

## Deleting Sessions

You can delete Sessions until the last Session of the course has ended. After that only Admins can delete them, because attendance and certificates depend on them.

## Telling participants about changes

When you change the time or the locations of a Session and people are registered, EduHub asks **Inform participants?** after saving. Click **Inform** to send the Session change notification: an email that says what changed and what the Session was before. **Don't inform** sends nothing. The email is never sent automatically.

Separately, every participant gets one Session reminder before the first Session, at the time set in the course's email templates, unless that setting is **Off**. It is sent only once, even if you move the first Session later.

## Turning a Session into a public event

**Public event** creates a draft event from a Session, for example for a talk that is open to everyone.
