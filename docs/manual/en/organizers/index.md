**Organizers** run courses: the instructors of a course, and the Org Admins of the organization that offers it. **Admins** have full access to all organizations.

| Role | What they do |
|------|--------------|
| Instructor | Works on their own courses: texts, Sessions, registrations, attendance. |
| Org Admin | Manages the programs of one organization (courses, events or degrees, as allowed): creates and publishes courses and changes their registration settings. |
| Admin | Can do everything, in every organization, including issuing certificates. |

You manage courses in two places:

- **Manage Courses** (menu item **Courses**, Org Admins and Admins): the list of all courses of a program. Here you create, copy and publish courses and change settings such as the registration type, price and certificates. Click a row to expand it.
- **The manage page of a course**: opened from the list or from the course page. It has the tabs **Description**, **Dates** (for events: **Programme**), the registrations tab (**Applications**, **Registrations** or **Registrations and Payments**) and **Participations**.

Admins also find the projects of all courses in one list under the menu item **Projects** (see [Projects overview](projects-overview.md)).

Instructors confirm a confidentiality notice the first time they open the manage page of a course.

The rules of your organization (for example deadlines or certificate requirements) are not part of this manual. If your organization publishes them online, a link to them appears at the bottom of every manual page.
