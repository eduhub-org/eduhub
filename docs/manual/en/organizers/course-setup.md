## Creating a course

Only Org Admins and Admins can create courses. Open **Manage Courses**, choose the program tab the course belongs to, and click **Add Course** (or **Add Event** / **Add Degree**). The course is created immediately as **Untitled Course**, with the program's default deadline and an online location. You then fill in everything else.

You can also let the [Assistant](assistant.md) create the course and draft its texts.

### Course titles must be unique

Within a course or degree program, every course needs its own title. Upper and lower case and spaces at the start or end don't count as a difference. If the title is taken, you see: "This program already has “…”. Titles must be unique within a program." Event programs are exempt: several events may have the same title.

## Texts and basic information

On the **Description** tab of the course's manage page you fill in:

- **Short Description** (up to 500 characters), shown at the top of the course page.
- **Info Block 1** and **Info Block 2**, each with a heading and text.
- **Weekday**, **Start Time** and **End Time**, if the course takes place regularly.
- **Language** of the course.
- **Maximum number of participants**. Leave it empty for no limit. When the course is full, people can only join the waitlist.
- **Location**: add a **Link or Address** per location with **Add location**, for example an online meeting or a room. An online meeting link is shown only to the course's participants (confirmed, registered or completed), its instructors, Org Admins allowed to manage the program and Admins; everyone else sees just "Online".

**Learning Goals** are only shown for courses with an Achievement Certificate.

## Settings in the course list

Org Admins and Admins change further settings in the expanded row of the course under **Manage Courses**:

- **Registration type**: switch on **External registration**, **Application process**, **Questionnaire** or **Payment**. An application process always includes a questionnaire and can't be combined with payment. The line **Registration type** sums up the result, for example "Direct Confirmation without Questionnaire".
- **Pricing Settings** for paid courses, with the **Base Price (in cents)**.
- **Participant chat**: whether the course has a course chat.
- **Show available places on the offering's page**.
- **Email Templates**, including when the Session reminder is sent: off, or 2 hours to 1 week before the first Session.
- **Award certificates**: which certificates the course can award (Attendance Certificate, Achievement Certificate), the ECTS credits and the **Maximum Missed Sessions**.

## Publishing

A course only appears in the catalogue once it is published. Under **Manage Courses**, select the course and choose **Publish Selected**; **Unpublish Selected** takes it offline again. A course or event can only be published once it has at least one instructor (for an event, an organizer). The **Published** column shows a green dot for published courses.
