You handle registrations on the registrations tab of the course's manage page. It is called **Applications**, **Registrations** or **Registrations and Payments**, depending on the registration type. Courses with an external registration link have no such tab.

## Rating applications

On an application course, rate each application in the **Evaluation** column:

- green **Invite**
- orange **Undecided**
- red **Reject**
- grey **Unrated**

The rating alone sends nothing. You decide when the applicants are told.

## Sending invitations and rejections

Click **Send Invitations and Rejections by Rating**. Applications rated green get an invitation and those rated red a rejection, as long as they have not been decided yet. Alternatively select people and choose **Invite Selected (green rating)** or **Reject Selected (red rating)**.

If there are fewer free places than green ratings, the waitlist goes first (in the order people joined it), then earlier applications. The rest are not invited, so the course is not overbooked by accident; the dialog tells you how many were held back.

### Invitation deadline

Every invitation has a last day, set in **Invitation deadline**. By default it is one week from today, but never later than the first day of the course. Invitations that are not accepted by then expire and their places are free again. **Renew Invitation** gives an invited person a new deadline.

## Moving people up from the waitlist

Moving up is always your decision; EduHub never does it automatically. In the row of a person on the waitlist:

- **Application course** or **paid course**: click **Invite now**. The person gets an invitation to confirm, or to pay, by the deadline.
- **Free course with direct registration**: click **Confirm participation**. The person is confirmed straight away.

Free places belong to the waitlist first. New people can only register directly again once the course has more free places than people waiting.

## Overbooking

You can give out more places than the course has, for example because you expect people to drop out. Before you do, EduHub warns you how many places you would overbook. People registering themselves can never overbook a course.

## Other actions

- **Cancel selected registrations** cancels registrations on someone's behalf.
- You can email people by status or rating from the tab, and export the participant list.
- Requests to cancel a paid registration reach you; you decide on the cancellation and any refund.
- Only Admins can add or edit participants by hand (**Add or Edit Participants**).
