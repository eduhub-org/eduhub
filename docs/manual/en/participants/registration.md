## Logging in

To register you need an EduHub account. If you are not logged in, the registration button takes you to the login page first. The only exceptions are courses that register you on another website and events that allow [registration without an account](#registering-without-an-account).

## Direct registration or application

Each course uses one of two ways of registering. The button on the course page tells you which one:

- **Register Now**: direct registration. You get your place without anyone having to decide. Some courses ask you a few questions first; others confirm you with one click.
- **Apply now**: an application. You fill in a short form or a **Motivation Letter**, and the Organizers decide whether you get a place.

Below the button you see the **Registration deadline** or the **Application deadline**. You can still register on that day; registration closes at midnight (German time).

If the course registers you on another website, the button reads **Register via External Link** and opens that website.

## After applying

Your application is now under review. The course page says **Your application is under review**, and you get an email confirming that your application was received.

The Organizers then decide. When they accept you, you get an **invitation** that you have to confirm by a deadline. See [Invitations and the waitlist](invitations-and-waitlist.md). If you are not accepted, the course page says **Unfortunately, your application has been rejected** and you get an email.

## After registering directly

Your registration is confirmed straight away and you get a confirmation email. When the course starts, the course page shows the buttons to the course chat and the online meeting, if the course has them.

## Paid courses

On a paid course the button reads **Register Now** and opens a summary of the price and any add-ons. Accept the terms and click **Proceed to Payment**. You then pay by card or by direct debit (SEPA). Your place is confirmed once the payment is completed.

- Your invoice is sent to you by email. With a direct debit the money is collected a few days later, and the invoice follows once it has been collected.
- If a payment was not completed, the course page shows **Retry Payment**.
- If a direct debit fails later, your registration is cancelled.

## When the course is full

When all places are taken, the button reads **Join Waitlist**. You can then put yourself on the waitlist. On a paid course you pay nothing at this point. See [Invitations and the waitlist](invitations-and-waitlist.md).

## Registering without an account

Some events let you register without an EduHub account. You then see **Register without an account** below the login button. Enter your name and email address and click **Send registration**.

You are only registered once you click the link in the confirmation email. The link is valid for 24 hours, at most until the event starts. Every email you receive contains a link where you can cancel your registration or delete your data.

Registering without an account is only possible for free events with direct registration, before the deadline and while places are free. You can't join a waitlist this way.
