## Invitations

An **invitation** is the offer of a place that is held for you until a deadline, the last day of the invitation. You get one when the Organizers accept your application or move you up from the waitlist. You are told by email, and the course page shows the invitation.

On the last day you get a reminder email. If you do not accept the invitation by then, it expires, you are told by email, and the place goes to someone else. The course page then says **Your invitation has expired**.

### Accepting an invitation

- **Free course:** open the course page. Check your details (for example occupation and organization) and click **Confirm participation**.
- **Paid course:** you accept the invitation by paying. Click **Pay now** on the course page and pay before the deadline shown there.

Once you have accepted, your registration is confirmed and you get a confirmation email.

### Declining an invitation

If you can't take part, decline the invitation so that someone else gets the place: click **Decline participation** or **Decline spot** on the course page and confirm. The place is free again and the Organizers are told.

## The waitlist

When a course is full you can join its **waitlist**. People on the waitlist are in the order in which they registered. The course page says **You are on the waitlist**, and you get an email.

- When a place becomes free, it goes to the waitlist first. New people can only register directly again once there are more free places than people waiting.
- Moving someone up from the waitlist is always a decision of the Organizers; it does not happen automatically.
- When you are moved up, you either get your place straight away (on a free course with direct registration) or you get an [invitation](#invitations) that you have to accept by its deadline.
- EduHub does not show your position on the waitlist.

## Cancelling your registration

As long as you have not paid anything, you can cancel your registration yourself on the course page:

- **Before the first Session:** click **Cancel registration**. You can register again later while registration is still open.
- **Once the course has started:** click **Abort participation**. This can't be undone.
- **After the course has ended** you can no longer cancel.

Once you have **paid** for a course, you can't cancel yourself. Click **Request cancellation** and, if you like, give a reason. A place on the waitlist of a paid course costs nothing yet, so you cancel it with **Cancel registration** as above. The Organizers decide on the cancellation and any refund. You can send this request only once.
