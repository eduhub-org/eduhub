If a course has a **course chat**, you talk to the other participants and the instructors there. Once your registration is confirmed, it appears on the course page under **Course chat**. Whether a course has a course chat is up to its Organizers.

The course chat is a room in the opencampus.sh chat. You write in it with your **Chat Account**, which belongs to your EduHub account. People outside opencampus.sh cannot join it.

## Connecting to the chat

The first time, click **Connect to the chat**. EduHub takes you to the chat's login page:

1. If you are not logged in there yet, log in with your EduHub account.
2. The page asks whether EduHub may open the chat for you. Confirm.

You then return to the course page and the chat. You only need to do this once in this browser.

If the login does not work, **Back to the course** takes you back to the course page, and **Try again** starts the login again.

If the login page offers someone else's account, for example on a shared computer, EduHub discards that login and shows a notice. Try again and choose another account on the login page.

## Writing and reading

- Type your message and click **Send** or press Enter. Shift+Enter starts a new line.
- **Load older messages** shows earlier messages.
- If a message could not be sent, it says **Not sent.** below it. **Send again** tries once more.
- You can **Delete** your own messages. They disappear for everyone; this cannot be undone.
- **Pinned** shows important messages the instructors keep at the top.
- The chat shows how many new messages you have not seen yet.

You see images, replies and edited messages in the course chat. You cannot send images, reply to a message or edit a message in EduHub; use the Element app for that.

The chat runs in one browser tab at a time. If it is already open in another tab, this one shows a notice; close the chat in the other tab and it appears here.

## The chat in the Element app

Element is the chat app of opencampus.sh. **GO TO CHAT** opens the same course chat there. You log in with the same EduHub account and write with the same Chat Account; all messages appear in both places.

In the list of your sessions in Element, EduHub shows up as a device of its own, for example "EduHub on Chrome for macOS". Element may show it as unverified. That is normal for EduHub, because the course chat is not encrypted.

## Logging out

When you log out of EduHub, EduHub also logs you out of the course chat in this browser. If you are also logged in to the Element app, you stay logged in there.
