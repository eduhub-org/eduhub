These pages explain how you take part in a course on EduHub: from registering or applying, through invitations and the waitlist, to attendance and certificates.

A **course** on EduHub can be a course, an event or a degree. They all work the same way: you register for it, and your **registration** then has a state, for example under review, invited, on the waitlist or confirmed. You see that state on the course page, next to the course description.

You find every course you have registered for under **My Courses** on the start page.
